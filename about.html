THE CHALLENGE

Finding a suitable role can take too much effort.

Job seekers often have to search across many sites and compare listings one by one. It can be difficult to tell which opportunities fit your skills, experience, and preferences.

01

Scattered job listings

Opportunities are spread across different job boards and company websites, making a focused search harder.

02

Repeated searching

Comparing every job description with your CV takes time and can make promising roles easy to miss.

03

Knowing what fits

A CV-based view of relevant skills and experience can help you assess which opportunities are worth pursuing.

OUR MISSION

Spend less time searching. Make your next move with confidence.

Our goal is to help job seekers turn their CV into a starting point for finding suitable work. AI is intended to surface relevant opportunities; you stay in control of which recommendations to explore and where to apply.

HOW IT WORKS

From your CV to opportunities to explore.

Submit your CV and role interest. The AI workflow analyses your profile, searches job platforms, and returns recommendations for you to review.

01
▤

Share your CV

Provide your CV and job preferences as the starting point for your search.

02
↗

CV analysis

The AI workflow identifies skills, experience, and qualifications in your CV.

03
✦

Search and match

The workflow searches external job websites and compares listings with your profile.

04
✓

Choose where to apply

Review recommended roles on the website, open a listing, and apply directly when you are ready.

Recommendations depend on the CV and listings available from the job platforms searched. You can review each result and follow its link to the original listing.

✦

RESPONSIBLE AI

AI can guide your search. You remain in control.

Recommendations are intended to help you find roles to consider, not to make decisions for you. Review each listing, check its details on the employer's site, and decide whether it fits your goals before applying.

Your choice matters
